# Privacy Policy for Colour Jam

**Last updated: 18 September 2026**

This policy explains what Colour Jam does with your information. It is short
because the game does very little.

## What the game stores

Colour Jam saves your progress — which levels you have finished, your best
move counts, your coins, your unlocked themes, and your settings — in local
storage **on your own device**.

That data never leaves your device. We cannot see it. It is not backed up to
us, it is not synced between your devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

## What we collect

**We collect nothing.** There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no
telemetry. The game does not send us any information about you or how you
play, and it works with no network connection at all.

## Advertising

Colour Jam shows occasional full-screen ads supplied by **Google AdMob**.

To serve those ads, Google may collect and process data, including your
device's advertising identifier. This processing is done by Google, not by us
— we never receive or store it. Google's practices are described in their
privacy policy:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543 (how Google uses advertising
  data)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the game shows
you Google's consent form before requesting any ad, and honours your choice.
If you decline, the game continues to work normally; you simply see fewer or
no personalised ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's
settings, under **Settings → Google → Ads**.

## Children

Colour Jam is rated for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at
children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children.

## Purchases

If you buy "Remove Ads", the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play.
We never see your payment details. The only thing stored is a flag on your own
device recording that the purchase was made.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to show,
correct or delete. To remove everything the game has stored, either use
**Settings → Reset progress** inside the app, or uninstall it.
