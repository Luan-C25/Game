# Privacy Policy for Glazeway

**Last updated: 16 November 2026**

This policy explains what Glazeway does with your information. It is short
because the game does very little.

## What the game stores

Glazeway saves your progress — which levels you have finished, your best
move counts, your coins, your unlocked themes, and your settings — in local
storage **on your own device**.

That data never leaves your device. We cannot see it. It is not backed up to
us, it is not synced between your devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

## What we collect

**From players, we collect nothing.** There is no account, no sign-in, no
analytics, and no telemetry. The game does not send us any information about
you or how you play, and it works with no network connection at all.

## Testing builds (Fourteen)

While Glazeway is in Google Play testing, the app includes the **Fourteen**
testing SDK. It does nothing unless you join the test through Fourteen or a
Fourteen invite link. If you do, it sends Fourteen a random tester ID (no name
or email), how long and how often you play, a count of touches, your phone
maker and model, Android version and country setting, whether the app came
from Google Play, crash details (the error type and where it happened, never
the error message), and any bug report you choose to send, with a screenshot
only if you attach one. It never reads your screen, typing, contacts,
location or advertising ID.

This is used only to confirm the game is being tested and to find bugs.
Fourteen's own policy is at https://getfourteen.app/privacy. The SDK is
removed before the game is released to everyone.

## Advertising

Glazeway shows occasional full-screen ads supplied by **Google AdMob**.

To serve those ads, Google may collect and process data, including your
device's advertising identifier. This processing is done by Google, not by us
— we never receive or store it. Google's practices are described in their
privacy policy:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543 (how Google uses advertising
  data)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the game shows
you Google's consent form before requesting any ad, and honours your choice.
If you decline, the game continues to work normally; you simply see fewer or
no personalised ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's
settings, under **Settings → Google → Ads**.

## Children

Glazeway is rated for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at
children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to show,
correct or delete. To remove everything the game has stored, either use
**Settings → Reset progress** inside the app, or uninstall it.
