## 1. What goes on an NDIS invoice

**Hook (on screen, first 2 seconds):** Your NDIS invoice got rejected. Here's why.

**Script:**
If you're an independent support worker, plan managers need five things to pay you. The participant's name. Their NDIS number. The date of each support. The support item number. And the price. Plus your ABN. Miss one and it bounces back. Put each shift on its own line with the date, item number, hours and rate.

**One tip:** One line per shift, not one line for the fortnight.

## 2. Who do you actually send it to?

**Hook:** Sending your NDIS invoice to the wrong person?

**Script:**
Ask your participant one question first: how is your plan managed? Plan-managed, the invoice goes to the plan manager, not the participant. Self-managed, it goes to the participant or their nominee. NDIA-managed, you don't send an invoice at all. Registered providers claim through the portal. Get this wrong and you wait weeks for nothing.

**One tip:** Get the plan manager's invoice email on day one.

**CTA:** The free template asks you this up front. Link in bio.

## 3. Your ABN on every invoice

**Hook:** No ABN on your invoice? That's expensive.

**Script:**
If you invoice a business without an ABN, they may have to hold back tax at the top rate. Plan managers will usually just reject it. An ABN is free. Put it on every invoice. And check it's typed right, one wrong digit and it fails. The free template checks your ABN against the ATO's check-digit rule as you type it.

**One tip:** Copy your ABN from the ABN Lookup site, don't type it from memory.

## 4. GST: probably not

**Hook:** Should you add GST to your NDIS invoice?

**Script:**
If you're not registered for GST, no. Don't add it and don't call it a tax invoice. Most sole traders earning under seventy-five thousand a year aren't registered. If you are registered, a lot of NDIS supports are GST-free when the conditions are met. That's a question for your accountant, not TikTok.

**One tip:** Not registered? Write "Not registered for GST" on the invoice.

**CTA:** The template leaves GST off unless you say you're registered.

## 5. Support item numbers

**Hook:** What's that 01_011_0107_1_1 thing?

**Script:**
That's a support item number. Every NDIS support has one, and plan managers need it on your invoice. This one's self-care support on a weekday during the day. The item depends on the type of support, the time and the day. They change, usually in July, so check the current NDIS pricing documents. Wrong item, wrong price, invoice comes back.

**One tip:** Check your items every July.

**CTA:** Free template has a spot for the item on every line. Link in bio.

## 6. When to chase an unpaid invoice

**Hook:** How long do you wait before chasing an NDIS invoice?

**Script:**
Here's a routine that doesn't burn bridges. Day after it's due: short, friendly email with the invoice attached. Seven days overdue: second email, ask if anything on it needs fixing. Fourteen days: pick up the phone. Most late invoices aren't anyone being dodgy. It went to the wrong inbox or something didn't match the plan.

**One tip:** Ask "does anything need fixing?" It gets a faster answer than "please pay".

**CTA:** Reminder wording you can copy is in the guide. Link in bio.

## 8. Kilometres you forgot to claim

**Hook:** You're probably not invoicing all your kilometres.

**Script:**
Quick run to the chemist mid-shift. Driving them home after an outing. A trip that changed on the day. If your service agreement covers travel, those kilometres can go on the invoice as their own line. But you have to log them. Do it before you start the car to go home.

**One tip:** Log km at the end of every shift, not at the end of the fortnight.

## 9. Kilometres at tax time

**Hook:** Two different kinds of kilometres. Don't mix them up.

**Script:**
One: kilometres you invoice the participant for, if your agreement covers it. Two: work travel you might claim at tax time. Driving between two participants is usually work travel. Home to your first participant is often treated as private. The ATO has a cents per kilometre method, capped at 5,000 kilometres. Ask your accountant how it applies to you.

**One tip:** Keep a log either way. It's your evidence.

**CTA:** Link in bio for the guide.

## 10. Setting aside tax

**Hook:** Nobody takes tax out of your NDIS pay. So you have to.

**Script:**
As a sole trader every invoice gets paid in full and the tax bill turns up later. Easy habit: every payment that lands, move a percentage into a separate account and don't touch it. A lot of people use somewhere between twenty and thirty percent. An accountant can give you the right number for your income.

**One tip:** Separate account. Out of sight.

**CTA:** ShiftBill shows what to set aside each time you mark an invoice paid. It's an estimate, not tax advice.

## 11. PAYG instalments

**Hook:** Got a letter from the ATO about instalments?

**Script:**
After your first year or so as a sole trader, the ATO may put you into PAYG instalments. That means paying towards next year's tax every quarter instead of one big bill. It's normal. If you've been setting money aside from every payment, the instalments are easy. If you haven't, start this week.

**One tip:** Treat the instalment like a bill you already saved for.

**CTA:** Guide on setting aside tax at the link in bio.

## 13. Invoice numbers

**Hook:** Stop numbering your invoices "Invoice 1".

**Script:**
Number them in order: INV-0001, INV-0002, and so on. Never reuse a number. Plan managers look for duplicates, and your accountant will thank you. Use the invoice number as the payment reference too, so you can see which invoice a payment was for.

**One tip:** Invoice number = payment reference.
