# Week one plan

Written 21 October 2026. Goal for week one: find out whether support workers will use the free template, and which channel brings them. Revenue in week one would be a bonus, not the test.

## Before day 1
Finish `LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md` up to "Go live (test mode)". Check on your own phone that you can make a template PDF, sign up, log a shift and make an invoice.

## Which channel first, and why
1. **TikTok first.** Search traffic will be slow for a new site on a `workers.dev` address competing with established free templates, so search is a month-two channel. TikTok can put a video in front of support workers on day one, with no followers needed, and several small apps already market to sole traders there.
2. **Reddit second**, slowly. r/NDIS does not allow advertising, so the first post there is pure help (post 2) to build trust and karma. The product post goes to r/NDIS_Providers a few days later.
3. **Search runs in the background.** Submit the sitemap to Google Search Console on day 1 (checklist item) and leave it.

## What to measure
Write these down on day 7 (and every Sunday after).

| Measure | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Visits to the template page | Cloudflare Web Analytics |
| Template PDFs made, by source | `/admin/`, column "Template PDFs" |
| Sign-ups, by source | `/admin/` |
| Checkouts started and paying users, by source | `/admin/` |
| Packs sold | `/admin/` |
| TikTok views, profile visits and link clicks, per video | TikTok analytics |
| Reddit upvotes, comments, removed or not | Reddit |

The useful ratios:
- **Template completion rate** = PDFs made / template page visits. Below 20% means the form is too long or confusing: watch someone use it.
- **Sign-up rate** = sign-ups / PDFs made.
- **Paid rate** = paying / sign-ups.

## Numbers that decide what happens next

These are starting thresholds, not science. Revisit them at the end of month one.

**After week one**
- Any channel with at least 10 template PDFs made: keep going on that channel.
- TikTok videos all under 300 views: change the hooks (try script 15 and 14 styles) before deciding TikTok does not work.
- A Reddit post removed: do not repost. Read the rule, wait a week, try a different subreddit.

**After four weeks (double down if)**
- 50 or more template PDFs a week, or
- 5 or more paying users from any one source.
Then: post more of whatever brought them, and consider the A$15 to A$25 domain from revenue so reminder emails can switch on.

**After eight weeks (stop or change course if)**
- fewer than 100 template PDFs a month in total, and
- fewer than 3 paying users,
with at least 4 posts a week across TikTok and Reddit in that time. At that point, stop adding features. Either keep the free template and pack running as they cost nothing, or pick a different audience (for example cleaners or gardeners who invoice NDIS plan managers) and test that instead.

**Price check**
- If the paid rate is above 10% of sign-ups, the price may be too low: test A$9 a month for new sign-ups.
- If people sign up and use the shift log but almost nobody pays, the free limit may be too generous or the paid plan is not worth it yet. Ask five of them why, directly.
