## Page 1: About your closed test

### How did you recruit users for your closed test?

I posted in Reddit tester communities and tested other developers' apps in return. Testers joined a Google Group, then opted in through Play. I didn't use a paid testing service or friends and family.

### How easy was it to recruit testers for your game?

Pick **Difficult** if it took a while to reach 12 testers, or **Neither
difficult or easy** if they joined quickly. Neither answer counts against you.

### Describe the engagement you received from testers during your closed test

Testers were most active in the first few days, some reaching level 28 and using undo, restart and theme unlocks. After that most opened it daily for under a minute, typical of reciprocal testers. Real players should play longer and go further into the 200 levels.

### Provide a summary of the feedback that you received from testers

Testers sent written comments. They liked the clean menu and free undo and restart, and found levels 26 to 28 challenging. They asked for a pause before the win screen, steadier fast swipes, cheaper themes, level chapters and colour-blind shapes on the gates.

## Page 2: About your game

### Who is the intended audience of your game?

Casual puzzle players aged 13 and up, especially people who like Block Jam style sliding puzzles but are put off by timers, lives and levels that feel impossible. It suits short sessions on a commute or a break, and players who want a calm game to pick up and put down.

### Describe what makes your game stand out

Every one of the 200 levels is checked by a solver before release, so none are impossible. Hints, undo and restart are free and unlimited, with no timers, lives or energy. Ads follow written limits shown in the game: none before level 10, never mid-puzzle. Colour-blind shapes are built in.

### How many installs do you expect your game to have in your first year?

**0 - 10K**

## Page 3: Your production readiness

### What changes did you make to your game based on what you learned during your closed test?

Testers said the win screen covered the board too quickly, so 1.0.1 adds a longer pause. Fast swipes went the wrong way, so drags now follow the finger and a flick sends a block out through its gate. Colour-blind shapes now show on gates, and themes are cheaper with five new ones.

### How did you decide that your game is ready for production?

All 200 levels are verified by a solver and replayed by automated tests on every build. Testers reached level 28 without crashes or blocking bugs, and I tested 1.0.1 on my own phone, tuning the new flick from 58 logged swipes. The ad limits and privacy policy are in place.
